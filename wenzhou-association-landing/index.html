关于我们About Us

他乡遇乡亲，异国如故里Finding home through community.

每一位离开家乡来到加拿大的温州人，都怀揣着不同的梦想。有人来求学，有人创业，有人陪伴家人，也有人在这里开启新的生活。 Every Wenzhou person who comes to Canada carries a different dream—some come to study, some to build a business, some to join family, and some to begin a new chapter.

虽然身在异国，但一句熟悉的乡音、一桌熟悉的家乡菜、一群彼此照应的乡亲，都能让人重新感受到家的温暖。温州同乡会正是在这样的初心下成立。 Even far from home, a familiar accent, a shared meal and the care of fellow Wenzhou people can bring back the warmth of belonging. That is why our association was created.

我们希望连接每一位温州乡亲，让大家在这里相识、相助、成长，也让温州文化、乡音与家乡记忆在加拿大继续延续。 We bring Wenzhou people together to connect, support one another and grow, while keeping our culture, language and memories alive in Canada.

会长寄语President's Message

愿每一位温州人，都能在这里感受到家的温暖May every Wenzhou person feel at home here.

欢迎每一位来自温州的乡亲。离开家乡，来到加拿大，我们都经历过陌生，也都感受过来自乡亲的一句问候、一份帮助所带来的温暖。 Welcome to every member of our Wenzhou community. Coming to Canada means facing the unfamiliar, but it also teaches us how much warmth can come from a simple greeting or a helping hand from someone who understands where we come from.

温州同乡会，希望成为这样一个地方。在这里，我们不仅分享资源，更分享信任；不仅传承文化，更传递乡情。愿每一位温州人在加拿大都能找到归属，结识朋友，互相扶持，共同成长。 We hope the Wenzhou Association becomes that kind of place. Here, we share not only resources, but trust; not only culture, but a deep sense of hometown connection. May every Wenzhou person in Canada find belonging, friendship and support.

— 温州同乡会 会长— President, Wenzhou Association of Ontario
